Chicken and potato meal-prep bowls use one roasting method and seven sauces to make dinner feel different through the week. Each bowl starts with a measured chicken portion, potatoes, and vegetables. Many can fit around 400–500 calories with the listed portions, but calculate from your own ingredients before relying on that limit.

These are cooking templates, without a claim that they are viral, kitchen-tested, or a guaranteed weight-loss plan. Add a larger potato portion or another side when you need more food.

## Base recipe: two bowls

Use 10 oz raw boneless skinless chicken breast, 12 oz potatoes, 2 cups broccoli or green beans, 2 tsp olive oil, and pepper. Cut potatoes into 1/2-inch pieces and chicken into even bite-size pieces. Heat the oven to 425°F.

Toss potatoes with 1 tsp oil and roast for 15 minutes. Add chicken and vegetables tossed with the remaining oil. Roast another 15–20 minutes, turning once, until chicken reaches 165°F and potatoes are tender. Divide into two bowls. Timing varies with the oven and size of the pieces.

The plain bowl is roughly 350–450 calories with about 30–40g protein, depending on the ingredients. Count sauces separately. Raw and cooked chicken weights are not interchangeable.

## Seven flavor combinations

The quantities below season one bowl. Keep wet toppings separate until serving for better texture.

1. **Lemon herb:** Add 1 tbsp lemon juice, 1/2 tsp dried oregano, and 2 tbsp plain Greek yogurt to the cooked bowl. Use green beans as the vegetable.
2. **Smoky barbecue:** Coat cooked chicken with 1 tbsp barbecue sauce and add 1/2 cup shredded cabbage. Check the sauce label rather than assuming all brands have the same sugar or calories.
3. **Buffalo ranch:** Toss chicken with 1 tsp hot sauce. Mix 2 tbsp plain yogurt with a pinch each of garlic powder, dill, and pepper. Serve with broccoli.
4. **Taco lime:** Season raw chicken with 1/2 tsp cumin and paprika before roasting. Finish with 2 tbsp salsa, 1 tbsp cilantro, and lime juice.
5. **Garlic Parmesan:** Add 1 tbsp finely grated Parmesan and a pinch of garlic powder after roasting. A little cheese spreads farther when grated finely.
6. **Mediterranean:** Top with 1/2 cup chopped cucumber and tomato, 1 tbsp crumbled feta, and 1 tbsp lemon juice. Count the extra vegetables and cheese in the final nutrition.
7. **Mustard dill:** Stir 1 tsp Dijon mustard into 2 tbsp plain yogurt with dill. Spoon over reheated chicken and potatoes rather than baking the yogurt sauce.

## Shopping list for seven bowls

- About 2 1/4 lb raw chicken breast and 2 3/4 lb potatoes.
- About 7 cups broccoli, green beans, or a mixture; extra cabbage, cucumber, or tomato for chosen variations.
- At least 7 tsp olive oil, plus yogurt, lemons, and seasonings.
- Buy only the sauces you will use. Two flavors are enough for a first batch.

## A two-session prep schedule

Make three bowls on Sunday and four on Wednesday, or freeze later cooked portions promptly. Cool in shallow containers and refrigerate within two hours. Use refrigerated cooked leftovers within 3–4 days and reheat to 165°F. Make yogurt dressings fresh for better texture.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I use thighs?** Yes. Check temperature and recalculate calories for the cut and any skin. The breast estimate does not automatically apply.

**Can I refrigerate seven bowls for seven days?** No. Make a second batch or freeze later portions within the safe storage window.
