Meal prep keeps healthy eating simple and consistent.

Start small:
- Pick 2 proteins, 2 carbs, and 2 veggies each week
- Batch cook once or twice; store in clear containers
- Use sauces/spices to create variety from the same base foods

Template ideas:
- Chicken + rice + broccoli
- Salmon + quinoa + spinach
- Eggs + potatoes + peppers

Time-savers:
- Sheet-pan meals; rice cooker; instant pot
- Pre-cut veggies or frozen options
- Make double portions for lunch leftovers

Success tip: plan, shop, prep, repeat. Consistency beats complexity.
