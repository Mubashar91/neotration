Eating healthy on a budget is doable with a simple process.

Core tactics:
- Plan 3–5 simple meals you repeat during the week
- Buy seasonal produce and frozen veggies
- Choose budget staples: oats, rice, beans, eggs, chicken thighs, yogurt
- Cook once, eat twice: batch cook proteins and grains

Shopping checklist:
- Pick a protein, a carb, and a veggie for each meal block
- Compare unit prices; stick to store brands when quality is similar
- Avoid snack aisles; make a list and shop your list

Use a calorie calculator to set targets and track portions without overbuying. Small constraints make smart budgets easier.
