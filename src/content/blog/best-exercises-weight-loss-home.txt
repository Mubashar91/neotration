## Why Home Workouts Work for Weight Loss

The best workout is the one you actually do consistently. For millions of Americans, getting to a gym is a genuine barrier — whether it's cost, time, distance, or comfort level. Home workouts eliminate all of those barriers. When your workout requires zero travel and no equipment, your only obstacle is motivation.

Combined with a proper calorie target from the FitJourney USA calculator, a consistent home workout routine can produce dramatic weight loss results.

## The Best Home Exercises for Fat Loss

### 1. Jumping Jacks
A classic full-body cardio move that burns approximately 8–10 calories per minute. Great for warming up or as a standalone cardio burst between strength exercises.

### 2. Burpees
The king of home fat-burning exercises. Burpees combine a squat, push-up, and jump into one explosive movement that works your entire body and burns 10–15 calories per minute. Start with 5 reps and build up.

### 3. High Knees
Run in place while driving your knees up to hip height. This elevates your heart rate quickly and burns significant calories while improving cardiovascular fitness.

### 4. Mountain Climbers
Start in a push-up position and alternate driving each knee toward your chest as fast as possible. Works your core, shoulders, and legs while keeping your heart rate high.

### 5. Bodyweight Squats
The most important lower-body exercise you can do without equipment. Squats work your quads, hamstrings, glutes, and core. Aim for 3 sets of 15–20 reps. As you get stronger, try jump squats for extra calorie burn.

### 6. Push-Ups
Work your chest, shoulders, triceps, and core. If standard push-ups are too hard, start on your knees. If they're too easy, elevate your feet on a chair.

### 7. Lunges
Step forward and lower your back knee toward the floor. Alternate legs for 3 sets of 12 reps per side. Walking lunges across the room add a cardio element.

### 8. Plank
Hold a push-up position with your body in a straight line. Builds core strength and stability. Start with 20–30 second holds and work up to 60+ seconds.

### 9. Glute Bridges
Lie on your back with knees bent, push your hips toward the ceiling and squeeze your glutes at the top. Excellent for activating the posterior chain and building lower body strength.

### 10. Jump Rope (or Simulated Jump Rope)
Jump rope burns more calories per minute than almost any other exercise — around 12–15 calories per minute. No rope? Simulate the motion with your wrists while jumping in place.

## Sample 30-Minute Home Fat-Loss Workout

Warm-up (5 minutes): Jumping jacks, arm circles, leg swings

Circuit (repeat 3 times, 60 seconds each exercise, 15 seconds rest):
Burpees → High knees → Bodyweight squats → Push-ups → Mountain climbers → Lunges

Cool-down (5 minutes): Walking in place, stretching

This workout burns approximately 250–400 calories depending on your body weight and intensity.

## How to Maximize Fat Loss With Exercise

Exercise alone rarely produces significant weight loss without dietary changes. The most effective approach is combining home workouts with a calorie deficit. Use the FitJourney USA calorie calculator to set your daily intake goal, then use exercise to increase your overall calorie burn.

Aim for at least 150 minutes of moderate exercise per week — that's just 30 minutes, five days a week.
