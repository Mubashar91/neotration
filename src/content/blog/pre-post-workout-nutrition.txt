Fuel timing improves training and recovery.

Before workout (60–90 min):
- Carbs + a bit of protein; lower fat for easier digestion
- Examples: yogurt with fruit; toast with egg; banana and whey

After workout (0–2 hours):
- Protein for repair + moderate carbs to replenish glycogen
- Examples: chicken and rice; tuna sandwich; smoothie with whey + oats + berries

Hydration & electrolytes:
- Aim for pale yellow urine; add sodium if you sweat heavily

Keep it simple: consistent meals that agree with your stomach beat exotic pre-workout stacks.
