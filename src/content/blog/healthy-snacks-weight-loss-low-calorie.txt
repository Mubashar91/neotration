Snacking often gets a bad reputation in weight loss circles, but the right snacks can actually help you lose weight by preventing overeating at meals and maintaining steady energy levels. The key is choosing nutrient-dense, low-calorie snacks that satisfy hunger without derailing your daily calorie goals.

## Why Snacking Can Help Weight Loss

Strategic snacking offers several benefits for weight management:

- **Prevents extreme hunger**: Avoiding long gaps between meals prevents ravenous overeating
- **Stabilizes blood sugar**: Prevents energy crashes that trigger cravings
- **Boosts metabolism**: Eating every 3-4 hours keeps your metabolic furnace burning
- **Supports muscle**: Protein-rich snacks help preserve lean mass during a deficit
- **Improves compliance**: Satisfying snacks make calorie restriction sustainable

## Top 25 Healthy Snacks Under 150 Calories

### High-Protein Snacks (100-150 calories)

**1. Greek Yogurt with Berries**

## Snacking Strategies for Weight Loss Success

### Timing Your Snacks

**Best snack times for hunger management:**

- **Mid-morning** (10-11 AM): Prevents lunch overeating
- **Mid-afternoon** (3-4 PM): Prevents dinner ravenous hunger
- **Pre-workout** (30-60 min before): Provides energy
- **Post-workout** (within 30 min): Supports recovery

### Pre-Portioning Is Key

Never eat directly from the package. Instead:

- Pre-portion snacks into 100-150 calorie servings
- Use small containers or baggies
- Keep pre-portioned snacks at eye level in the fridge
- Log snacks immediately in your calorie tracker

### The Protein + Fiber Formula

The most satisfying snacks combine protein AND fiber:

**Examples:**
- Greek yogurt (protein) + berries (fiber)
- Hummus (protein) + vegetables (fiber)
- Apple (fiber) + nut butter (protein/fat)
- Hard-boiled egg (protein) + cherry tomatoes (fiber)

## Snacks to Avoid During Weight Loss

**High-calorie, low-satisfaction options:**

- Chips and crackers (easy to overeat, low nutrition)
- Candy and chocolate bars (blood sugar spike and crash)
- Granola and trail mix (calorie-dense, small portions)
- Dried fruit (concentrated sugar, easy to overeat)
- Smoothies and juices (liquid calories don't satisfy)
- Energy bars (often candy bars in disguise)

## Smart Snacking by Scenario
