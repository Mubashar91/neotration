America’s grocery shelves are full of nutrient-dense choices.

Top picks:
1) Blueberries – antioxidants for brain health
2) Salmon – omega‑3s for heart and recovery
3) Greek yogurt – protein + probiotics
4) Leafy greens – vitamins A, C, K + fiber
5) Eggs – complete protein at low cost
6) Beans and lentils – fiber, protein, and budget‑friendly
7) Oats – slow energy and cholesterol support
8) Nuts – healthy fats and minerals
9) Sweet potatoes – beta‑carotene and fiber
10) Broccoli – vitamin C and sulforaphane

Easy ways to use them:
- Smoothies with yogurt + berries + spinach
- Grain bowls with salmon, rice, and broccoli
- Omelet with spinach and cheese
- Chili with beans and sweet potato

Make 1–2 swaps a week and build your superfood habits steadily.
