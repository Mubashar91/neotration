Anti-inflammatory eating is one of the fastest-growing healthy-food searches on Pinterest heading into 2026, and sheet-pan dinners are the easiest way to put it into practice. One pan, 30 minutes, and almost no cleanup.

There's no single "anti-inflammatory diet." The term describes an eating pattern rich in vegetables, fruit, fatty fish, olive oil, beans, nuts, whole grains, herbs, and spices, and low in ultra-processed foods and added sugars. That's very close to the Mediterranean pattern, which has the strongest research behind it. These 15 dinners follow that pattern and include approximate calories and protein per serving.

> This article is general nutrition information. Food choices support overall health but aren't a treatment for any medical condition.

## The Sheet-Pan Formula

Almost every recipe below follows the same template, so once you learn it you can improvise:

1. **Heat the oven to 425°F (220°C).** Line a large sheet pan with parchment.
2. **Protein:** 1–1¼ lb (450–565g) for 4 servings
3. **Vegetables:** 6–8 cups, cut to similar sizes
4. **Fat:** 1–2 tbsp extra-virgin olive oil total
5. **Flavor:** spices (turmeric, ginger, garlic, cumin, paprika), herbs, and citrus
6. **Roast 20–25 minutes,** adding fast-cooking items (fish, shrimp, greens) partway through

## 15 Anti-Inflammatory Sheet Pan Dinners

### 1. Lemon-Herb Salmon with Broccoli & Cherry Tomatoes
~380 calories | 32g protein. Roast broccoli for 10 minutes, add salmon fillets and tomatoes, and roast 12 more minutes. Finish with lemon and dill.

### 2. Turmeric Chicken with Cauliflower & Chickpeas
~420 calories | 38g protein. Toss chicken thighs, cauliflower, and canned chickpeas with turmeric, cumin, garlic, and olive oil. Roast 25 minutes.

### 3. Mediterranean Shrimp with Zucchini & Peppers
~290 calories | 28g protein. Roast the vegetables for 12 minutes, then add shrimp tossed with oregano and garlic for the final 8 minutes.

### 4. Ginger-Garlic Tofu with Bok Choy & Mushrooms
~310 calories | 20g protein. Press firm tofu, cube it, and toss with ginger, garlic, and low-sodium soy sauce. Roast 25 minutes, adding bok choy for the last 8.

### 5. Sweet Potato, Black Bean & Kale Hash with Eggs
~360 calories | 18g protein. Roast sweet potato cubes for 15 minutes, add black beans and kale, then crack eggs into wells and bake 8 minutes.

### 6. Cod with Olives, Tomatoes & Capers
~260 calories | 30g protein. Lay cod on a bed of cherry tomatoes, olives, and capers. Roast 15 minutes and serve with crusty whole-grain bread.

### 7. Chicken Sausage with Brussels Sprouts & Apples
~370 calories | 24g protein. Halve the Brussels sprouts, slice apples and chicken sausage, and roast together with olive oil and thyme.

### 8. Harissa Chickpeas with Carrots & Red Onion
~330 calories | 13g protein. Serve over Greek yogurt for +10g protein. A good vegetarian Meatless Monday option.

### 9. Sheet-Pan Fajitas with Turkey or Chicken
~350 calories | 33g protein. Peppers, onions, and sliced chicken with cumin and smoked paprika. Serve in corn tortillas or over greens.

### 10. Miso-Glazed Salmon with Green Beans
~370 calories | 31g protein. Brush salmon with white miso, a little honey, and rice vinegar. Roast with green beans for 12–14 minutes.

### 11. Moroccan-Spiced Chicken with Butternut Squash
~410 calories | 36g protein. Cinnamon, cumin, and coriander on chicken and squash cubes. Top with parsley and pomegranate seeds.

### 12. Sardine & White Bean Tomato Bake
~340 calories | 26g protein. Canned sardines are one of the cheapest sources of omega-3 fats. Bake them with white beans, tomatoes, garlic, and spinach.

### 13. Tempeh with Broccoli & Peanut-Lime Drizzle
~380 calories | 24g protein. Roast tempeh and broccoli, then drizzle with a mix of peanut butter, lime, and water.

### 14. Lemon-Garlic Chicken with Asparagus & Potatoes
~400 calories | 35g protein. Roast baby potatoes for 10 minutes first, then add chicken breast and asparagus.

### 15. Rainbow Veggie & Lentil Grain Bowls
~420 calories | 19g protein. Roast beets, carrots, and red onion, then serve over cooked lentils and farro with tahini-lemon dressing.

## Anti-Inflammatory Flavor Boosters

- **Turmeric + black pepper:** pepper helps your body absorb curcumin
- **Fresh ginger and garlic:** strong flavor, almost zero calories
- **Extra-virgin olive oil:** the main fat in the Mediterranean pattern
- **Citrus and fresh herbs:** brighten roasted dishes after cooking
- **Walnuts or pumpkin seeds:** add crunch and healthy fats

## Meal Prep Tips

- Cook two pans at once, one for dinner and one for lunches
- Roasted vegetables and chicken keep 4 days refrigerated
- Cook fish fresh where possible because it doesn't reheat as well
- Store sauces separately so vegetables stay crisp

For more prep strategies, see our [meal prep for beginners guide](/blog/meal-prep-beginners-guide).

## The Bottom Line

You don't need special supplements or exotic ingredients to eat in an anti-inflammatory way. A sheet pan, plenty of colorful vegetables, fish a couple of times a week, beans, olive oil, and spices will get you there in 30 minutes on a weeknight.
