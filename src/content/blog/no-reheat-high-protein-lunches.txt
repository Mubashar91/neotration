No-reheat lunches help when work has no microwave or you would rather eat a cold meal. These ten lunch boxes combine a main protein, vegetables or fruit, and a grain or bean component. Most can reach around 30g protein with the listed portions, but package labels determine the actual total.

No reheating does not mean no refrigeration. Pack perishable lunches with cold packs and refrigerate on arrival where possible. Food left warm all morning is not made safe by eating it cold.

## Ten lunch boxes for one

1. **Chicken chickpea crunch:** Pack 3 oz cooked chicken, 1/2 cup chickpeas, 1 cup cucumber and tomato, and 1 tbsp lemon dressing. Keep a whole-grain pita separate. Chicken and chickpeas supply roughly 30g protein before the pita.
2. **Tuna white bean box:** Mix one drained 5 oz can light tuna, 1/2 cup white beans, 1 cup celery and cucumber, and 1 tsp olive oil with lemon. Add crackers separately. Check drained tuna weight and its label.
3. **Turkey hummus wrap:** Fill a whole-grain wrap with 4 oz cooked turkey, 3 tbsp hummus, lettuce, and grated carrot. Turkey and wrap brands differ in protein and sodium.
4. **Tofu edamame bowl:** Pack 6 oz ready-to-eat baked tofu, 1/2 cup cooked shelled edamame, 1/2 cup cooked rice, and 1 cup cabbage. Dress with 1 tsp sesame oil and rice vinegar. Follow tofu cooking instructions if not ready to eat.
5. **Egg cottage cheese bento:** Pack 2 fully cooked hard-boiled eggs, 3/4 cup cottage cheese, cherry tomatoes, and whole-grain crackers. Keep eggs chilled and cheese in its own container.
6. **Chicken pasta salad:** Toss 3 oz cooked chicken, 1/2 cup cooked pasta, 1 cup peas and peppers, and 2 tbsp Greek yogurt with lemon. Cool cooked ingredients promptly before mixing.
7. **Salmon lentil lunch:** Combine 4 oz cooked salmon, 1/2 cup cooked lentils, 1 cup cucumber and greens, and lemon. Cook fish to 145°F, cool promptly, and transport cold.
8. **Savory yogurt box:** Pack 1 cup plain Greek yogurt with herbs as a dip, 2 oz cooked chicken strips, carrots, cucumber, and one small pita.
9. **Chicken salsa bowl:** Layer 3 oz cooked chicken, 1/2 cup black beans, 1/2 cup corn, lettuce, and 2 tbsp salsa. Add lime before eating and pack chips separately if wanted.
10. **Cottage cheese edamame plate:** Pair 1 cup cottage cheese with 1/2 cup cooked edamame, tomato, and rye crispbread. Check the dairy and soy labels for your protein total.

## Prep two or three boxes at a time

Choose combinations with overlapping ingredients: chicken chickpea and chicken pasta share chicken; tofu edamame and cottage cheese edamame share edamame. Cook and cool grains promptly, wash produce, and portion dressing separately.

Sunday prep should cover the first part of the week. Make a second batch midweek or freeze suitable components early. Cucumber, lettuce, and dressed yogurt salads do not freeze well; add after thawing.

## Shopping list

- Pick two main proteins, such as chicken and tofu, plus yogurt or cottage cheese if needed.
- Buy beans and tuna only if your chosen boxes use them.
- Choose one grain: wraps, rice, pasta, pita, or crackers.
- Add three vegetables, one dressing base, and cold packs for transport.

## Frequently asked questions

**How long can lunch stay unrefrigerated?** Perishable food should generally be refrigerated within two hours, or one hour above 90°F. Cold packs should maintain a safe temperature during transport.

**Is 30g protein necessary at lunch?** No. It is a meal-planning example, not a universal requirement.
