Sustainable weight loss follows simple, repeatable rules.

Pillars:
- Calorie deficit: small and steady (300–500 cal/day)
- Protein target: ~0.7–1.0 g per lb bodyweight
- Strength training: 2–4x/week to preserve muscle
- Steps: 7–10k/day, or a consistent baseline you can keep
- Sleep: 7–9 hours to support appetite and recovery

Weekly rhythm:
- Plan meals Sunday, shop once, prep basics (protein + grain + veggies)
- Track calories honestly; adjust by ±100–200 if weight stalls 2+ weeks
- Weigh 3–4x/week and track a weekly average, not daily noise

Mindsets that work:
- Consistency over intensity
- Don’t let a bad meal become a bad week
- Focus on behaviors you control, not scale fluctuations

Sustainability wins. Small changes, compounding over time, create big results.
