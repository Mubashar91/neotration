December is slow-cooker season. Dump dinners are the easiest kind: you put raw ingredients straight into the pot with no browning or prep beyond opening a few cans, press start, and dinner is ready when you get home. These 10 recipes are built around lean protein, with 30–45g per serving and most under 450 calories.

Each recipe serves 4–6 and works in a standard 6-quart slow cooker.

## Food Safety Basics First

- **Don't cook frozen meat in a slow cooker.** It can sit too long in the unsafe temperature zone. Thaw it in the fridge first.
- **Keep the lid on.** Each lift adds roughly 15–20 minutes of cooking time.
- **Check doneness:** chicken and turkey at 165°F (74°C), beef and pork at 145°F (63°C) for whole cuts or 160°F (71°C) ground.
- **Refrigerate leftovers within 2 hours.**

## 10 High-Protein Slow Cooker Dump Dinners

### 1. Salsa Chicken (Shreddable)
**Dump:** 2 lb boneless skinless chicken breast, 1 jar (16 oz) salsa, 1 packet taco seasoning (or 2 tbsp homemade)
**Cook:** Low 6 hours / High 3 hours, then shred
**Per serving (6):** ~240 calories | 36g protein
Serve in lettuce wraps, burrito bowls, or over rice.

### 2. White Chicken Chili
**Dump:** 2 lb chicken breast, 2 cans white beans (drained), 1 can diced green chiles, 1 diced onion, 3 cups chicken broth, 2 tsp cumin
**Cook:** Low 7 hours. Shred chicken and stir in ½ cup Greek yogurt.
**Per serving (6):** ~360 calories | 44g protein

### 3. Turkey Taco Lentil Soup
**Dump:** 1 lb lean ground turkey (crumbled raw), 1 cup dry red lentils, 1 can diced tomatoes, 1 jar salsa, 4 cups broth, taco seasoning
**Cook:** Low 6–7 hours. Stir to break up turkey.
**Per serving (6):** ~330 calories | 31g protein

### 4. Honey-Garlic Chicken Thighs
**Dump:** 2 lb boneless skinless thighs, ¼ cup low-sodium soy sauce, 2 tbsp honey, 4 cloves garlic, 1 tbsp rice vinegar, 1 tsp ginger
**Cook:** Low 5–6 hours. Thicken sauce with 1 tbsp cornstarch slurry.
**Per serving (6):** ~290 calories | 31g protein
Serve over steamed broccoli and rice.

### 5. Lean Beef & Bean Chili
**Dump:** 1½ lb 93% lean ground beef (crumbled raw), 2 cans kidney beans, 1 can crushed tomatoes, 1 diced onion, 1 diced bell pepper, 2 tbsp chili powder
**Cook:** Low 8 hours
**Per serving (6):** ~380 calories | 35g protein

### 6. Chicken Tikka Masala (Lightened)
**Dump:** 2 lb chicken breast (cubed), 1 can (15 oz) tomato sauce, 1 diced onion, 3 cloves garlic, 2 tbsp garam masala, 1 tsp turmeric
**Cook:** Low 6 hours. Stir in ¾ cup Greek yogurt and ½ cup light coconut milk at the end.
**Per serving (6):** ~300 calories | 40g protein

### 7. Pork Tenderloin with Apples
**Dump:** 2 lb pork tenderloin, 2 sliced apples, 1 sliced onion, ½ cup broth, 1 tbsp Dijon mustard, 1 tsp thyme
**Cook:** Low 5–6 hours (to 145°F), rest and slice
**Per serving (6):** ~260 calories | 33g protein

### 8. Buffalo Chicken (for Wraps or Bowls)
**Dump:** 2 lb chicken breast, ½ cup buffalo sauce, ½ cup broth, 1 tsp garlic powder
**Cook:** Low 6 hours. Shred and stir in ¼ cup Greek yogurt plus a little ranch seasoning.
**Per serving (6):** ~230 calories | 36g protein

### 9. Italian Turkey Meatball Soup
**Dump:** 1 lb frozen-then-thawed turkey meatballs, 1 can white beans, 1 can diced tomatoes, 4 cups broth, 3 cups spinach (add last 30 min), Italian seasoning
**Cook:** Low 6 hours
**Per serving (5):** ~320 calories | 28g protein

### 10. Black Bean & Quinoa Chicken Burrito Bowls
**Dump:** 1½ lb chicken breast, 1 cup uncooked quinoa (rinsed), 1 can black beans, 1 can diced tomatoes with chiles, 1 cup corn, 2 cups broth, cumin and chili powder
**Cook:** Low 5 hours. Shred chicken and stir.
**Per serving (6):** ~410 calories | 38g protein

## How to Make Any Dump Dinner Higher in Protein

- **Stir in Greek yogurt at the end** in place of sour cream or cream (+5–8g per serving)
- **Add a can of beans or ½ cup of dry lentils** for extra protein and fiber
- **Use chicken breast or 93%+ lean ground meat** for more protein per calorie
- **Serve over cottage cheese mashed potatoes** or with a side of edamame

## Freezer Dump Bags for Busy Weeks

Most of these recipes work as freezer kits. Add everything **except dairy** to a labeled gallon freezer bag and freeze for up to 3 months. Thaw overnight in the fridge, dump into the slow cooker in the morning, and stir in the yogurt or coconut milk at the end.

## The Bottom Line

Slow-cooker dump dinners are the lowest-effort way to eat high-protein meals in winter. Pick two recipes, cook a batch on Sunday, and you have warm, 30g+ protein dinners and lunches ready for most of the week.
