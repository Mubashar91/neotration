Hydration is more than just “eight glasses a day.”

Common myths, debunked:
- Everyone needs 8 cups: needs vary with body size, climate, diet, and activity.
- Clear urine always best: pale yellow is ideal; crystal clear may indicate overhydration.
- Only water counts: milk, tea, fruits, soups, and high‑water foods contribute.
- Electrolytes are only for athletes: heavy sweaters and hot climates may benefit.

Practical targets:
- Start with ~30–35 ml per kg bodyweight per day; add more if you sweat.
- Sip steadily across the day; front‑load in the morning.
- Include sodium and potassium if training long or sweating heavily.

Checks:
- Thirst, urine color, and energy are quick feedback signals.
- Don’t chug liters at once; spread intake to avoid discomfort.
