Macros are your energy sources—protein, carbohydrates, and fats.

Quick definitions:
- Protein: recovery, muscle repair, satiety
- Carbs: primary training fuel, support performance
- Fats: hormones, vitamin absorption, long-lasting energy

Balanced plate idea:
- 1–2 palms protein, 1–2 cupped hands carbs, 1–2 thumbs fats, and plenty of veggies

Adjust by goal:
- Lose: slightly more protein and veggies; modest carb/fat reduction
- Gain: add 200–300 calories from quality carbs and fats; keep protein steady
- Maintain: stay near maintenance; keep meals consistent

Track weekly trends and adjust portions gradually.
