Protein ice cream starts with a milk-and-yogurt base, then changes flavor through fruit, cocoa, coffee, or spices. These ten Ninja Creami pint ideas share one base, with a blender-and-freezer alternative for readers without the machine. They are dessert recipes, not a weight-loss requirement.

Use your model's manual for its fill line, compatible containers, freezing position, and processing settings. A standard pint recipe must not be assumed to fit every Creami container. These variations are recipe suggestions, without a claim of kitchen testing or brand endorsement.

## The base for one standard pint

Whisk 1 cup low-fat milk, 1/2 cup plain nonfat Greek yogurt, 1 tbsp maple syrup, and 1/2 tsp vanilla until smooth. Add the chosen flavor below. Leave room below your model's maximum fill line; reserve excess mixture in a separate food-safe container.

Depending on brands, the plain base is roughly 230–280 calories and 20–25g protein for the whole mixture. Flavor additions change those estimates. Calculate from your milk, yogurt, and syrup labels; a pint is not necessarily one serving. Protein powder is optional and not included in these estimates.

## Ten protein ice cream flavors

1. **Vanilla bean:** Use the base as written with an extra 1/4 tsp vanilla or a pinch of vanilla seeds.
2. **Chocolate:** Whisk in 1 tbsp unsweetened cocoa and 1 tbsp extra milk before freezing; taste for sweetness before it freezes.
3. **Strawberry:** Blend in 1/2 cup strawberries. Reduce milk to 3/4 cup to leave room for the fruit.
4. **Blueberry lemon:** Blend 1/3 cup blueberries and 1/2 tsp lemon zest into the base; reduce milk by 1/4 cup.
5. **Coffee:** Dissolve 1 tsp instant coffee in 1 tbsp warm milk, cool, and whisk into the base. Choose decaf if preferred.
6. **Peanut butter:** Whisk in 1 tbsp powdered peanut butter plus 1 tbsp milk. Check its label for sweeteners and peanut allergens.
7. **Banana cinnamon:** Blend in 1/2 small ripe banana and 1/4 tsp cinnamon, reducing milk by 1/4 cup.
8. **Peach vanilla:** Blend 1/2 cup thawed peaches with the base; reduce milk to 3/4 cup.
9. **Mint chocolate:** Add just 1 drop food-grade peppermint extract before freezing, then adjust next time if needed. Stir in 1 tsp mini chocolate chips after processing.
10. **Pumpkin spice:** Whisk in 1/4 cup plain pumpkin puree and 1/4 tsp pumpkin pie spice, reducing milk by 2 tbsp.

## Creami method

Mix or blend a completely smooth base. Freeze in the approved container on a level surface for the time specified in your manual, commonly at least 24 hours. Use the setting recommended for that mixture. If crumbly, follow the manual's guidance for adding a little liquid and reprocessing.

Do not process loose hard ice, solid frozen fruit chunks, or an angled frozen block contrary to the manufacturer's instructions. Add mix-ins only at the appropriate stage. Wash parts as instructed and keep dairy mixtures refrigerated until freezing.

## No-Creami method

Pour the smooth base into a shallow freezer-safe container. Freeze until firm but still scoopable, stirring every 30–45 minutes during the first few hours to break up crystals. A low-fat base becomes harder and icier than churned ice cream; let it soften briefly before scooping.

For a soft-serve texture, freeze the mixture in a suitable ice-cube tray and blend in small batches with a splash of milk, only if your blender is rated for frozen ingredients. Serve immediately or refreeze in a covered container.

## Shopping and storage

- Buy milk, plain Greek yogurt, maple syrup, vanilla, and the flavor ingredients for two or three pints first.
- Label containers with flavor, preparation date, and allergens. Milk, peanuts, and optional powders need clear labeling in a shared freezer.
- Keep frozen at 0°F. For texture, make small batches and use within a few weeks; quality and safety are different questions.

## Frequently asked questions

**Do I need protein powder?** No. Milk and Greek yogurt already contribute protein.

**Are all ten pints under 300 calories?** Not necessarily. Brands and additions differ; use labels and count toppings separately.
