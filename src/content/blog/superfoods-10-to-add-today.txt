# 10 Superfoods to Add to Your Diet Today

Superfoods are nutrient powerhouses that provide exceptional health benefits. Here are ten to add to your weekly rotation — with simple ways to use each.

## 1) Blueberries
Packed with antioxidants supporting brain and heart health. 
Use: Smoothies, yogurt bowls, oatmeal, snack.

## 2) Spinach
Loaded with iron, vitamins A/C/K, and calcium.
Use: Salads, omelets, smoothies, sautéed side.

## 3) Salmon
Rich in omega‑3s for heart and brain health.
Use: Baked fillets, salmon bowls, canned salmon wraps.

## 4) Greek Yogurt
High in protein and probiotics for gut health and fullness.
Use: Parfaits, smoothies, savory dips.

## 5) Quinoa
Complete protein, naturally gluten‑free, high in fiber.
Use: Grain bowls, salads, as rice substitute.

## 6) Avocados
Healthy fats that aid nutrient absorption and satiety.
Use: Toast, salads, smoothies, guacamole.

## 7) Sweet Potatoes
High in fiber and beta‑carotene for eye health.
Use: Baked wedges, mash, sheet‑pan meals.

## 8) Almonds
Provide healthy fats, protein, vitamin E.
Use: Snacks, yogurt topping, homemade trail mix.

## 9) Chia Seeds
Omega‑3s, fiber, and protein in tiny packages.
Use: Chia pudding, smoothie thickener, overnight oats.

## 10) Green Tea
Antioxidants and a gentle caffeine boost.
Use: Hot, iced, or matcha lattes.

## How to Start (Without Overhauling Your Diet)
- Add 1–2 superfoods per week
- Keep them visible in your fridge/pantry
- Use simple swaps (quinoa for rice, Greek yogurt for mayo)

Small, consistent additions compound into big health benefits.
