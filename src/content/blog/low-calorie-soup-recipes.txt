Low calorie soups are one of the most effective tools for eating satisfying, filling meals without spending a lot of your daily calorie budget. A large bowl of vegetable-based soup can run 80–150 calories while delivering real volume, fiber, and warmth. This guide covers seven soups with calories per bowl, plus tips for making each one more filling.

Soup works for calorie control because of its high water content. Research from Penn State found that eating soup before a meal reduced total calorie intake by 20% compared to eating the same ingredients as a solid food. The volume and warmth signal fullness faster.

## 1. Classic Vegetable Soup (~90 calories per bowl)

The most versatile low-calorie soup. You can make a huge pot for under $10 and eat it all week.

**Ingredients (serves 6):**
- 2 cups diced tomatoes (canned or fresh)
- 2 cups green beans, chopped
- 2 cups zucchini, diced
- 1 cup carrots, diced
- 1 cup celery, sliced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 4 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:** Sauté onion, celery, and carrots for 5 minutes. Add garlic, then remaining vegetables and broth. Simmer 20 minutes.

**Per bowl (1.5 cups): ~90 calories | 4g protein | 18g carbs | 1g fat | 5g fiber**

## 2. Chicken and Vegetable Soup (~150 calories per bowl)

Adding chicken transforms vegetable soup into a complete, protein-rich meal.

**Ingredients (serves 6):**
- 1 lb chicken breast, cooked and shredded
- 3 cups mixed vegetables (carrots, celery, peas)
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 cups low-sodium chicken broth
- 1 tsp thyme
- 1 bay leaf
- Salt and pepper

**Instructions:** Simmer all ingredients together for 25 minutes. Remove bay leaf before serving.

**Per bowl (1.5 cups): ~150 calories | 22g protein | 10g carbs | 2g fat**

This is one of the most filling soups on the list because of the high protein content. Great for lunch or a light dinner.

## 3. Lentil Soup (~180 calories per bowl)

Lentils are one of the highest-fiber, highest-protein plant foods available. This soup is thick, hearty, and surprisingly filling for its calorie count.

**Ingredients (serves 6):**
- 1.5 cups dry green or brown lentils
- 1 can diced tomatoes (14 oz)
- 2 carrots, diced
- 2 stalks celery, sliced
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 5 cups low-sodium vegetable broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- Juice of 1 lemon

**Instructions:** Sauté vegetables 5 minutes. Add lentils, tomatoes, broth, and spices. Simmer 30–35 minutes until lentils are tender. Stir in lemon juice before serving.

**Per bowl (1.5 cups): ~180 calories | 12g protein | 32g carbs | 1g fat | 10g fiber**

## 4. Tomato Basil Soup (~110 calories per bowl)

A classic comfort soup that's naturally low in calories. Pair with a small piece of whole grain bread for a complete meal.

**Ingredients (serves 4):**
- 2 cans whole peeled tomatoes (28 oz each)
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 4 cloves garlic, minced
- 1 cup low-sodium vegetable broth
- ¼ cup fresh basil leaves
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- Salt, pepper, and a pinch of sugar

**Instructions:** Sauté onion and garlic in olive oil. Add tomatoes and broth. Simmer 20 minutes. Blend until smooth. Stir in fresh basil.

**Per bowl (1.5 cups): ~110 calories | 3g protein | 18g carbs | 3g fat | 4g fiber**

## 5. Cabbage Soup (~80 calories per bowl)

One of the lowest-calorie soups you can make. Extremely filling due to the high fiber and volume of cabbage.

**Ingredients (serves 6):**
- ½ head green cabbage, chopped
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 3 stalks celery, sliced
- 2 carrots, diced
- 1 onion, diced
- 4 cups low-sodium broth
- 1 tsp Italian seasoning

**Instructions:** Sauté vegetables 5 minutes. Add cabbage, tomatoes, and broth. Simmer 25 minutes.

**Per bowl (1.5 cups): ~80 calories | 3g protein | 16g carbs | 0.5g fat | 4g fiber**

## 6. Black Bean Soup (~200 calories per bowl)

Thick, creamy, and high in both protein and fiber. This soup is satisfying enough to be a complete meal.

**Ingredients (serves 4):**
- 2 cans black beans (15 oz each), drained and rinsed
- 1 can diced tomatoes
- 1 medium onion, diced
- 3 cloves garlic, minced
- 2 cups low-sodium chicken or vegetable broth
- 1 tsp cumin
- 1 tsp chili powder
- Juice of 1 lime

**Instructions:** Sauté onion and garlic. Add beans, tomatoes, broth, and spices. Simmer 20 minutes. Blend half the soup for a creamy texture, then stir back in.

**Per bowl (1.5 cups): ~200 calories | 12g protein | 36g carbs | 1g fat | 12g fiber**

## 7. Miso Soup with Tofu (~70 calories per bowl)

The lightest soup on the list. Japanese miso soup is traditionally served as a starter and is extremely low in calories while providing gut-friendly probiotics from the miso paste.

**Ingredients (serves 4):**
- 4 cups water
- 3 tbsp white miso paste
- 4 oz firm tofu, cubed
- 2 green onions, sliced
- 1 cup baby spinach
- Optional: 1 sheet nori, cut into strips

**Instructions:** Bring water to a simmer (do not boil). Whisk in miso paste. Add tofu, spinach, and green onions. Heat 2 minutes. Serve immediately.

**Per bowl (1 cup): ~70 calories | 6g protein | 6g carbs | 2g fat**

## Tips for Making Soups More Filling

Add protein: stir in shredded chicken, canned tuna, or white beans to any vegetable soup. Add fiber: throw in a handful of spinach, kale, or lentils. Use broth wisely: low-sodium broth adds flavor without significant calories. Eat slowly: soup eaten slowly signals fullness more effectively than quickly consumed solid food.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: Are soups good for weight loss?**
Soups are excellent for calorie-controlled eating because their high water content creates volume and satiety at low calorie cost. Broth-based soups are significantly better than cream-based soups for this purpose.

**Q: How many calories should a meal be?**
For most people targeting 1,500–1,800 calories per day, a main meal should be 400–600 calories. These soups work well as lighter meals or as starters before a protein-focused main course.

**Q: Can I freeze homemade soup?**
Yes. Most soups freeze well for up to 3 months. Let cool completely, portion into freezer-safe containers, and label with the date. Thaw overnight in the refrigerator.

**Q: How do I add more protein to vegetable soup?**
Add 1 cup cooked white beans (+15g protein, +150 calories), 1 cup shredded chicken (+35g protein, +165 calories), or 4 oz firm tofu (+10g protein, +80 calories).

**Q: What's the lowest-calorie soup?**
Miso soup (~70 cal/bowl) and cabbage soup (~80 cal/bowl) are the lowest-calorie options on this list. Both are filling due to their high water and fiber content.
