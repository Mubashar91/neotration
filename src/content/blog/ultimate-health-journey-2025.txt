Starting a health journey is about consistency over perfection.

In this guide, you'll learn how to:
- Set specific, realistic goals (e.g., “Walk 20 minutes, 5 days a week”).
- Choose a nutrition approach you can stick to (not the most extreme one).
- Track the big three: calories, protein, and steps.
- Measure progress beyond the scale: energy, sleep, mood, and waist.

Week-by-week starter plan:
- Week 1: Log food honestly; set maintenance calories; walk 3–5x.
- Week 2: Add a small deficit (≈300–500 cal/day) or surplus if gaining.
- Week 3: Hit protein target (0.7–1.0 g/lb bodyweight); keep steps consistent.
- Week 4+: Keep routines simple; review progress and adjust by ±150–200 cal.

Mindset:
- Most results come from a few habits done well. Be patient, and build momentum with small daily wins.
