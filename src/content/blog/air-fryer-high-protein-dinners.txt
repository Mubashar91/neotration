These six air fryer dinners pair a measured protein portion with vegetables and a simple side. Each aims for a roughly 20-minute cooking window with prepared ingredients; preheating, chopping, appliance size, and cooking in batches can add time. A thermometer matters more than a timer.

With the portions listed, many combinations can fit around 400–500 calories, but they are not verified nutrition analyses. Count your actual ingredients, oil, sauces, and sides before using a calorie ceiling. Increase portions if you need a larger meal.

## Before you start

Use a single layer with space for airflow. Keep raw poultry and seafood away from ready-to-eat ingredients. Check the thickest piece with a food thermometer: poultry needs 165°F, fish 145°F, and ground turkey 165°F. Cook shrimp until pearly or white and opaque. Appliance times below are starting ranges.

## Six dinners for one

1. **Lemon chicken and broccoli:** Toss 5 oz raw diced chicken and 1 1/2 cups broccoli with 1 tsp olive oil, lemon zest, and pepper. Air fry at 380°F for about 12–16 minutes, shaking once and checking chicken reaches 165°F. Serve with 1/2 cup cooked rice.
2. **Salmon green bean plate:** Brush 5 oz salmon with 1 tsp olive oil and mustard. Cook alongside 1 cup green beans at 390°F for about 8–12 minutes; remove only when the fish reaches 145°F. Add a small precooked potato, reheated separately.
3. **Turkey burger bowl:** Form 5 oz lean ground turkey with pepper and garlic powder into a thin patty. Air fry at 375°F for about 12–16 minutes to 165°F. Serve over 2 cups chopped lettuce and tomato with a small whole-grain bun and 1 tbsp yogurt dressing.
4. **Paprika shrimp tacos:** Toss 6 oz peeled raw shrimp with 1 tsp oil and 1/2 tsp smoked paprika. Air fry at 390°F for about 6–9 minutes until opaque. Divide between two small corn tortillas with 1 cup cabbage and 2 tbsp plain yogurt.
5. **Tofu edamame bowl:** Pat dry 7 oz firm tofu and cut into cubes. Toss with 1 tsp oil and 1 tsp reduced-sodium soy sauce; air fry at 390°F for 12–18 minutes. Serve with 1/2 cup cooked shelled edamame and 1 cup broccoli. Check the tofu label for protein.
6. **Chicken fajita plate:** Mix 5 oz raw chicken strips, 1 sliced bell pepper, 1/4 onion, 1 tsp oil, and cumin. Air fry at 380°F for about 12–16 minutes to 165°F, stirring halfway. Serve with two small corn tortillas and 2 tbsp salsa.

## Shopping list for three dinners

Pick three recipes before shopping. For the chicken, salmon, and tofu options, buy 5 oz raw chicken, one 5 oz salmon fillet, one package tofu, broccoli, green beans, a potato, rice, and frozen shelled edamame. Pantry oil, mustard, lemon, and spices cover the seasonings.

Buy larger protein packages only if you can freeze unused portions promptly. A lower unit price does not help if half the food spoils. See our [budget meal prep plan](/blog/budget-high-protein-meal-prep) for a shared-ingredient basket.

## Prep ahead safely

Chop vegetables and measure sauces in advance, then cook the protein close to dinner. Store raw meat below produce in the refrigerator and observe its shorter raw storage limit. Freeze raw chicken you will not use within 1–2 days.

Refrigerate cooked leftovers promptly in shallow containers, use within 3–4 days, and reheat to 165°F. An air fryer can dry out lean chicken during reheating; cover or microwave with a little water if your appliance instructions allow.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I put everything in the basket together?** Only if it fits in a loose layer and you can check the protein separately. Crowding often requires longer cooking or a second batch.

**Does air frying make food low calorie?** No. Portions, oil, breading, toppings, and sides determine the meal's energy.

**What if I do not own an air fryer?** Use an oven-safe tray and suitable oven temperature, allowing extra time and checking food temperature. See [sheet-pan dinners](/blog/anti-inflammatory-sheet-pan-dinners).
