A simple 4‑week strength plan to build confidence and momentum.

Structure:
- 3 days/week full‑body (e.g., Mon/Wed/Fri)
- Focus on form, consistency, and small progressions

Core lifts:
1) Squat or goblet squat – 3 x 8–10
2) Hinge (deadlift or hip hinge) – 3 x 6–8
3) Push (push‑ups or bench press) – 3 x 6–10
4) Pull (rows or assisted pull‑ups) – 3 x 8–12
5) Core (plank or dead bug) – 3 x 20–40 sec

Progression:
- Week 1: learn technique, leave 2–3 reps in reserve
- Week 2: add 2.5–5% weight or 1–2 reps per set
- Week 3: repeat increments if form holds
- Week 4: maintain load; focus on perfect form and range of motion

Support:
- Protein: 0.7–1.0 g/lb bodyweight; sleep 7–9 hours
- Steps or light cardio on rest days for recovery

Goal: finish each session feeling capable, not crushed. Consistency compounds.
