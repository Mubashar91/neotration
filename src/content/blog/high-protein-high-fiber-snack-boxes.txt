Protein and fiber snack boxes pair a protein food with fruit, vegetables, beans, or whole grains. These twelve combinations are afternoon snacks or small eating occasions, generally below 300 calories using the listed portions. They are not necessarily a full lunch.

Check labels for crackers, hummus, tofu, cheese, and yogurt. If appetite is low during GLP-1 treatment, discuss your overall intake with your clinician; this list does not prescribe a medication-specific diet.

Fiber comes mainly from produce, legumes, seeds, oats, and crackers. These boxes differ in fiber content; the article is not claiming every box meets a regulated high-fiber labeling threshold.

## Assemble in ten minutes

Wash produce, drain beans, and portion protein first. Put yogurt and hummus into lidded pots. Keep crackers, oats, and seeds separate until eating if you prefer crunch. Cook eggs until whites and yolks are firm, then chill promptly.

Choose two combinations rather than preparing all twelve. Yogurt berry and yogurt oat share yogurt; egg edamame and edamame orange share edamame. Follow package cooking instructions for frozen edamame before chilling.

## Shopping list for four boxes

- One 16 oz tub plain Greek yogurt or cottage cheese.
- Two extras such as eggs, tofu, or canned light tuna.
- Two fruits and two vegetables you already enjoy.
- One fiber addition: chia, whole-grain crackers, oats, or chickpeas.
- Small containers and cold packs for transport.

## Store and travel safely

Keep dairy, chicken, tofu, eggs, and prepared produce at 40°F or below. Follow the shortest storage limit of any ingredient. Use a conservative 3–4-day meal-prep window for mixed boxes and assemble a second batch midweek.

Never leave a perishable box in a desk drawer or warm car. Refrigerate within two hours, or one hour above 90°F, unless cold packs maintain safe temperature. A snack box is a serving format, not shelf-stable packaging.

## Frequently asked questions

**Does every snack need protein?** No. These are options for people who want it, not a rule for every eating occasion.

**Can these replace meals?** A box under 300 calories may be inadequate as a regular meal. Discuss persistent low intake with a qualified professional.
