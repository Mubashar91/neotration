Budget high-protein meal prep works best when several dinners share a small shopping list. This seven-dinner plan uses chicken, beans, lentils, eggs, rice, and frozen vegetables. The worksheet aims for less than $5 per serving, but it is a planning budget, not a live Aldi price check or a guarantee about your store.

Prices vary by location, package size, and promotions. Enter your own receipt prices before shopping. The examples count ingredients used, while the checkout total includes whole packages and may be higher.

## Seven dinners from one basket

1. **Monday: chicken rice bowl.** Roast 5 oz raw chicken with 1 cup vegetables and 1 tsp oil at 425°F. Serve with 1/2 cup cooked rice; poultry must reach 165°F.
2. **Tuesday: lentil chicken soup.** Simmer 1/4 cup dry lentils, 1/2 can tomatoes, 1/2 cup carrots, and 1 1/2 cups water until lentils soften. Add 3 oz cooked chicken and heat thoroughly.
3. **Wednesday: black bean egg skillet.** Warm 3/4 cup beans with 1/2 cup tomatoes and 1/2 cup vegetables. Make two wells, add 2 eggs, cover, and cook until whites and yolks are firm. Serve with rice if wanted.
4. **Thursday: yogurt chicken plate.** Bake 5 oz raw chicken to 165°F. Serve with 1 cup roasted vegetables, 1/2 cup rice, and 2 tbsp yogurt mixed with pepper and garlic powder.
5. **Friday: lentil bean chili.** Simmer 1/4 cup dry lentils, 1/2 cup beans, 1/2 can tomatoes, onion, chili powder, and enough water until tender. Spoon 2 tbsp yogurt on top.
6. **Saturday: chicken fried rice.** In 1 tsp oil, cook 1 cup vegetables, add 1/2 cup cooked rice and 3 oz cooked chicken, and stir through 1 beaten egg. Cook the egg fully and heat leftovers to 165°F.
7. **Sunday: tomato chicken rice pot.** Cook 5 oz raw diced chicken with onion; add 1/2 cup tomatoes, 1/2 cup cooked rice, and 1 cup vegetables. Cover and simmer until the chicken reaches 165°F.

## Prep without wasting food

Cook the first half of the chicken and rice early in the week. Freeze later portions promptly, or keep later raw chicken frozen and thaw it in the refrigerator before cooking. Dry lentils need no overnight soaking; cook a small batch to use in soup and chili.

Do not store all seven cooked dinners in the refrigerator from Sunday onward. Use cooked refrigerated leftovers within 3–4 days. For a different batch system, try [rotisserie chicken meal prep](/blog/rotisserie-chicken-meal-prep) or [freezer meal bags](/blog/freezer-meal-prep-weight-loss).

## Recalculate your own cost

For each ingredient, divide package price by usable servings and multiply by the amount used. Include oil, sauces, toppings, and food discarded during prep. Cost per serving is the total recipe cost divided by the actual number of portions. Nutrition needs the same care: protein varies with the chicken cut, bean quantity, and yogurt brand.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is Aldi required?** No. This is a store-brand shopping framework you can use at another grocery store.

**Can I replace chicken?** Use tofu or extra beans if that costs less locally. Recalculate protein and cooking instructions rather than treating the swap as nutritionally identical.
