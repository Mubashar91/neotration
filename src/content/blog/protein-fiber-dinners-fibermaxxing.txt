"Fibermaxxing" is one of 2026's breakout food trends, with fiber being called "the next protein." There's real reason behind it. Most American adults eat only about 15g of fiber a day, while the Dietary Guidelines recommend about 14g per 1,000 calories, or roughly 25–34g for most adults.

Protein and fiber are the two nutrients that help most with feeling full after a meal. Protein slows digestion and supports muscle, and fiber adds bulk and keeps blood sugar steadier. Put both on the same plate and you get dinners that actually keep you satisfied.

Every dinner below delivers **at least 25g protein and 10g fiber**, most under 550 calories.

## Fibermaxxing Done Right

- **Increase gradually.** Add about 5g of fiber per day each week. Jumping from 15g to 40g overnight often causes bloating and gas.
- **Drink more water** as fiber goes up, because fiber needs fluid to work well.
- **Mix fiber types:** beans, oats, and fruit (soluble) plus vegetables, whole grains, and seeds (insoluble).
- **Get it from food first.** Whole foods bring vitamins, minerals, and plant compounds that fiber supplements don't.

## Easy Fiber Add-Ons (Any Dinner)

| Add this | Fiber added | Calories |
|---|---|---|
| ½ cup black beans | ~7.5g | ~110 |
| ½ cup lentils (cooked) | ~8g | ~115 |
| 1 tbsp chia seeds | ~4g | ~60 |
| 1 cup broccoli (cooked) | ~5g | ~55 |
| ½ cup raspberries (dessert) | ~4g | ~32 |
| Swap white pasta for chickpea pasta (2 oz) | +~5g | similar |

## Sample Fibermaxxing Day (~1,700 cal | 120g protein | 38g fiber)

- **Breakfast:** Overnight oats with Greek yogurt, chia, and raspberries (~30g protein, 12g fiber)
- **Lunch:** Leftover turkey and white bean chili (~38g protein, 12g fiber)
- **Snack:** Apple + string cheese (~7g protein, 4g fiber)
- **Dinner:** Chicken and black bean burrito bowl (~45g protein, 14g fiber)

## The Bottom Line

Fibermaxxing works best when it's paired with protein, not chasing a fiber number alone. Build dinners around a lean protein plus beans, lentils, whole grains, or a big serving of vegetables, raise your fiber intake slowly, and drink plenty of water.
