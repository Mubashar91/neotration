A January reset can mean making dinner easier instead of starting a restrictive diet. This fourteen-day rotation repeats ingredients and cooking methods while changing flavors. It includes a shopping framework and a second-week top-up, with portions you can adjust.

These are dinners, not a complete daily eating plan. Breakfast, lunch, snacks, activity, and personal needs all matter. There are no detox claims, promised pounds lost, or fixed daily calorie limits.

## Fourteen dinners

| Day | Dinner | How to make one serving |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Lemon chicken rice | Roast 5 oz raw chicken and 1 cup broccoli with 1 tsp oil; serve with 1/2 cup cooked rice |
| 2 | Lentil tomato soup | Simmer 3/4 cup cooked lentils, 1/2 cup tomatoes, 1 cup broth, and carrots until tender |
| 3 | Salmon potato plate | Roast 5 oz salmon, one small cubed potato, and green beans; start potatoes first |
| 4 | Tofu edamame | Cook 6 oz tofu, 1/2 cup edamame, and 1 cup vegetables with 1 tsp oil and soy sauce |
| 5 | Chicken bean tacos | Fill two small tortillas with 3 oz cooked chicken, 1/4 cup black beans, cabbage, and salsa |
| 6 | Turkey pasta | Cook 5 oz ground turkey to 165°F; add 1/2 cup tomatoes and serve with 2 oz dry pasta, cooked |
| 7 | Egg bean skillet | Warm 1/2 cup white beans and tomatoes; add 2 eggs and cook covered until firm |
| 8 | Mustard chicken bowl | Roast chicken, potatoes, and broccoli; finish with 2 tbsp yogurt and 1 tsp mustard |
| 9 | Chickpea tofu pan | Simmer 6 oz tofu, 1/2 cup chickpeas, 1/2 cup tomatoes, and spinach until hot |
| 10 | Salmon rice bowl | Cook fresh salmon; serve with 1/2 cup cooked rice, cucumber, and lemon |
| 11 | Chicken soup | Heat 3 oz cooked chicken, 1/2 cup beans, vegetables, and broth to 165°F |
| 12 | Peanut tofu noodles | Toss 6 oz tofu, cabbage, and noodles with 1 tbsp peanut butter thinned with water and soy sauce |
| 13 | Turkey chili | Simmer cooked ground turkey, 1/2 cup beans, tomatoes, peppers, and chili powder |
| 14 | Flexible leftovers | Use safely stored or frozen portions; add a fresh vegetable and grain as needed |

Cook all poultry, including ground turkey, to 165°F and salmon to 145°F. Dense vegetables may need more time than fish; start them first or use separate trays.

## Week-one shopping list

- Protein: chicken for two dinners, one salmon fillet, one tofu package, one turkey portion, and eggs.
- Legumes: cooked lentils, black beans, white beans, and frozen edamame.
- Starches: rice, potatoes, tortillas, and pasta.
- Produce: broccoli, carrots, green beans, cabbage, peppers, spinach, and lemons.
- Pantry: tomatoes, broth, olive oil, soy sauce, and seasonings.

Count household servings before buying. This list identifies ingredients rather than a precise grocery order for every family.

## Week-two top-up

Replace fresh fish, chicken, yogurt, cucumber, and depleted vegetables. Reuse rice, pasta, canned beans, tomatoes, and seasonings. Freeze spare raw proteins early instead of holding them refrigerated until week two.

Cook grains twice a week. Refrigerate leftovers promptly and use within 3–4 days or freeze later portions. Day fourteen is not permission to eat food refrigerated for two weeks.

## Make it a habit you can keep

Keep a backup meal frozen and write tomorrow's dinner before finishing today's cleanup. If plans change, return to the rotation without compensating by skipping meals. Repeat favorites next month instead of constantly finding new recipes.

## Frequently asked questions

**Is this a low-calorie diet?** No. Portions are flexible and dinners alone do not determine daily intake. Choose enough food for your circumstances.

**Must I start January 1?** No. Start whenever planning two weeks of dinners would help.
